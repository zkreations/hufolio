Blockquotes emphasize notable excerpts and external perspectives. Hufolio renders quotations with a subtle left accent line and muted typographic styling.

## Standard Blockquote

> Good design is as little design as possible. Less, but better — because it concentrates on the essential aspects, and the products are not burdened with non-essentials.
>
> — Dieter Rams, Ten Principles for Good Design

## Nested Quotation

Quotations can also be nested to show dialogue or layered citations:

> Digital minimalism requires continuous discipline in deciding what not to include.
>
> > When in doubt, leave it out. The content itself should provide the character, not the chrome surrounding it.
> >
> > — Editorial Reflections

Telegram Instant View preserves standard blockquote indentation and styling faithfully.
