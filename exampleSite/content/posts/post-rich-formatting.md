Editorial content frequently makes use of varied text treatments to indicate emphasis, corrections, and citations.

## Formatting Styles

- **Bold Text:** Used for **strong semantic emphasis** and crucial takeaways.
- *Italic Text:* Used for *gentle typographic inflection*, book titles, and foreign phrases.
- ***Bold and Italic:*** Combines ***both styles*** when extraordinary prominence is required.
- ~~Strikethrough:~~ Used to mark ~~obsolete information~~ or editorial revisions.
- Combined Inline: Writers can seamlessly combine **bold keywords** with *italicized explanations* and `inline code references`.

All formatting remains visually cohesive under both light and dark color schemes.
