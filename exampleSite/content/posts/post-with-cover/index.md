This article demonstrates how Hufolio handles featured cover images associated via Page Bundles.

The image `cover.jpg` is located directly alongside this Markdown document. In the web view, it renders immediately beneath the article header. In Telegram Instant View, the cover rule extracts the image and displays it at the top of the mobile preview.

Cover images should be purposeful and editorial, avoiding decorative stock visuals that provide no informational context.
